      Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours, one playthrough

      Guides put the platinum at difficulty 4/10 and about 200 hours: 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours on the 100% checklist, and 10 hours of Strangers & Freaks and clean-up. There are no online trophies, no difficulty settings, no New Game+, and no mission replay — but free-roam continues after the story, and everything for the platinum stays available there. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots): you cannot replay a mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off a trophy run.

      ⚠ The one trophy you can miss

      Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy the guides flag as potentially missable. The first meditation, at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, can only be done in Chapter 1: after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning — she says she is going to meditate — follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It adds +1 to your counter even though it is not marked as a Meditation Spot, and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required. Do it anyway: it costs a minute, and skipping it risks the platinum.

      Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap · also GTABase

      What the 100% checklist actually asks for (A Long Road)

      The checklist unlocks after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is far more forgiving than true 100% — roughly 60% of everything:

      • Every robbery: all 39 Illegal Gang Operations, all 8 large bank jobs, all 22 bank jobs and 7 Payday Loan jobs
      • Every underwater discovery, every shooting range gold, every drone race, all 15 of Bret’s Target Decks
      • Any 70 of the 80 challenges (skip the hardest 10)
      • Any 100 optional missions (there are 200–300+; each meditation counts +2 if done with both characters)
      • Scan any 200 of 277 base-model vehicles; study any 100 of 255 animals with the binoculars
      • 300 stunts; 15 of 18 meditations

      Source: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap · KeenGamer
